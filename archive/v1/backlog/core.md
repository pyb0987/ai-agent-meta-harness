### 86. P3 review governance complexity against the minimal outer-loop principle

Status: 대기

Source review: 2026-05-06 multi-review of clean local `main` against the
Meta-Harness methodology, excluding live-runtime evidence gaps.

The repository intentionally has richer local governance than the paper-core
Meta-Harness loop: score thresholds, VETO handling, fallback-threshold
dispositions, archive lifecycle checks, CI/release tiers, and multiple backlog
state transitions. Most of this is labeled as repository maintenance discipline,
not paper methodology. The remaining risk is operational complexity: the local
governance layer may become harder to explain and maintain than the simple
propose -> evaluate -> log loop it is meant to protect.

Potential improvement:

- Audit `MAINTENANCE.md`, backlog workflow, release gates, and multi-review
  guidance for rules that duplicate each other or no longer prevent a current
  failure mode.
- Produce a short governance map that separates paper-core loop, repository
  release discipline, advisory review-quality signals, and runtime-adapter
  tactics.
- Consider removing, merging, or demoting rules that add process weight without
  improving evaluator-boundary protection, trace reuse, or release reliability.
- Keep strong mechanical gates where they prevent known regressions; this item is
  not a request to weaken real safeguards.

Done when:

- A maintainer can explain the repository governance flow in one pass without
  confusing paper-core methodology with local release discipline.
- Any redundant or stale governance rule is removed, merged, or explicitly kept
  with a current failure-mode rationale.
- Future methodology reviews can judge governance complexity as an accepted
  maintenance trade-off rather than an untracked drift risk.
